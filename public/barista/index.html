Barista Express Impress

Cleaning & descaling care — phone-friendly checklist + log

Cleaning cycle

Back-flushing — separate from descaling. CLEAN / DESCALE light flashes when a cleaning cycle is required.

    Descaling

    Remove the water filter before adding descaling powder. Do not use bottled water when descaling.

    CLEAN / DESCALE light illuminates solid when descaling is required.

      Water filter

      Replacing every three months reduces the need to descale. In hard water areas, change more frequently.
